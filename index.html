🧠 The Science of Passive vs. Active Vocabulary

Most language learners face the same frustrating barrier: they understand movies, books, and podcasts easily (passive vocabulary), but when it's time to speak or write, the words simply do not come (active vocabulary). Vocabulary Activator was engineered specifically to solve this problem through contextual retrieval and spaced activation.

🎯 Why the Strict 10-Word Daily Limit?

Psycholinguistic research shows that the human working memory can only deeply assimilate 5 to 10 new language chunks per day. Adding 40 or 50 words creates cognitive overload and shallow familiarity without genuine activation. At 10 words a day, you activate up to 300 rich phrases a month—enough to completely transform your spoken fluency without burning out.

⏳ The 2 to 3 Day Spaced Activation Cycle

Words you save do not appear immediately. They appear 2 to 3 days later, right when your brain begins to let go of the memory trace. Retrieving the phrase at this critical threshold strengthens the neural pathways necessary for spontaneous speech.

🛡️ The Anti-Anki Pileup Guarantee

In conventional apps, missing a few days results in an overwhelming mountain of 50 to 100 cards, creating anxiety and guilt. In Vocabulary Activator, sessions are strictly capped at 10 items. If you missed Monday, you will only see Monday's 10 words when you return. No guilt, no chores—just steady, calm progress.

🔢 The 1 to 5 Activation Scale Explained

1
1 - Do Not Recognize: You have no recollection of this phrase. It will cycle back soon with fresh context.
2
2 - Recognize a Bit: The word looks vaguely familiar, but you cannot deduce its meaning without the definition.
3
3 - Know Meaning, Cannot Use: You understand it passively when reading, but cannot formulate your own sentence using it spontaneously.
4
4 - Can Use, Want Extra Practice: You feel you can use it, but want one more round of practice to feel 100% confident.
5
5 - Fully Activated: The chunk has become second nature. You can produce it immediately without hesitating.

The 2x Graduation Rule: When you rate a word '5' twice across distinct sessions, it is permanently promoted to your Active Vocabulary and retires from review sessions.
